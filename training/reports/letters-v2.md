# Letter model v2: training report

Generated by `scripts/train_letters.py`. 24 static letters (J and Z need motion, M6).

## Data

- Our recordings: 102 takes, 6068 frames (every 2nd frame used).
- asl-now (`sid220/asl-now-fingerspelling`, MIT): 1874 samples.
  - Orientation, inferred per sample by the label-free chirality model: 55.9% were right hands as given, the rest were flipped. 97.7% of decisions had confidence above 0.9. See `letters-v2-orientation.html`.
  - Aspect ratio (not recorded by the dataset): 4:3, the best of 4:3 51.8%, 16:9 44.1%, 1:1 49.8% (accuracy of an ours-trained model on asl-now).

## Does asl-now help on your held-out takes?

Cross-validated by take: every take is held out once, and models never see frames from a held-out take. Probabilities are averaged over seeds 0, 1, 2.

| Training data | Mean accuracy (single model) | Per seed | Seed ensemble |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ours only | 85.9% | 83.2%, 88.5%, 86.1% | 86.3% |
| Ours + asl-now | 94.8% | 94.3%, 95.8%, 94.2% | 95.1% |

**Shipped: ours + asl-now** (combined did not lower mean held-out accuracy). Per-letter results below use the seed ensemble for stability.

## Other signers

- Trained on ours, tested on asl-now: 51.8%
- Trained on asl-now, tested on ours: 81.4%
