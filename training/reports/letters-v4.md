# Letter model v4: training report

Generated by `scripts/train_letters.py`. 24 static letters (J and Z need motion, M6).

## Data

- Our recordings: 126 takes, 7502 frames (every 2nd frame used). 11 takes left out by `excluded_takes.json`.
- asl-now (`sid220/asl-now-fingerspelling`, MIT): 1874 samples.
  - Orientation, inferred per sample by the label-free chirality model: 55.3% were right hands as given, the rest were flipped. 97.4% of decisions had confidence above 0.9. See `letters-v4-orientation.html`.
  - Aspect ratio (not recorded by the dataset): 4:3, the best of 4:3 54.6%, 16:9 50.1%, 1:1 53.0% (accuracy of an ours-trained model on asl-now).

## Does asl-now help on your held-out takes?

Cross-validated by take: every take is held out once, and models never see frames from a held-out take. Probabilities are averaged over seeds 0, 1, 2.

| Training data | Mean accuracy (single model) | Per seed | Seed ensemble |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ours only | 88.6% | 87.5%, 90.5%, 87.8% | 89.1% |
| Ours + asl-now | 96.0% | 96.2%, 97.7%, 94.2% | 96.3% |

**Shipped: ours + asl-now** (combined did not lower mean held-out accuracy). Per-letter results below use the seed ensemble for stability.

## Other signers

- Trained on asl-now, tested on ours: 82.0%
