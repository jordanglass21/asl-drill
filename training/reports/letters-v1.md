# Letter model v1: training report

Generated by `scripts/train_letters.py`. 24 static letters (J and Z need motion, M6).

## Data

- Our recordings: 72 takes, 4309 frames (every 2nd frame used).
- asl-now (`sid220/asl-now-fingerspelling`, MIT): 1874 samples.
  - Orientation, inferred per sample by the label-free chirality model: 55.9% were right hands as given, the rest were flipped. 97.1% of decisions had confidence above 0.9. See `letters-v1-orientation.html`.
  - Aspect ratio (not recorded by the dataset): 1:1, the best of 4:3 52.8%, 16:9 44.9%, 1:1 53.3% (accuracy of an ours-trained model on asl-now).

## Does asl-now help on your held-out takes?

Cross-validated by take: every take is held out once, and models never see frames from a held-out take. Probabilities are averaged over seeds 0, 1, 2.

| Training data | Mean accuracy (single model) | Per seed | Seed ensemble |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ours only | 77.7% | 76.5%, 79.9%, 76.7% | 79.2% |
| Ours + asl-now | 91.5% | 91.0%, 94.6%, 88.8% | 92.0% |

**Shipped: ours + asl-now** (combined did not lower mean held-out accuracy). Per-letter results below use the seed ensemble for stability.

## Other signers

- Trained on ours, tested on asl-now: 53.3%
- Trained on asl-now, tested on ours: 79.9%
