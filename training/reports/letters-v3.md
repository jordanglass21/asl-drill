# Letter model v3: training report

Generated by `scripts/train_letters.py`. 24 static letters (J and Z need motion, M6).

## Data

- Our recordings: 127 takes, 7563 frames (every 2nd frame used).
- asl-now (`sid220/asl-now-fingerspelling`, MIT): 1874 samples.
  - Orientation, inferred per sample by the label-free chirality model: 55.9% were right hands as given, the rest were flipped. 97.8% of decisions had confidence above 0.9. See `letters-v3-orientation.html`.
  - Aspect ratio (not recorded by the dataset): 4:3, the best of 4:3 53.2%, 16:9 47.3%, 1:1 51.3% (accuracy of an ours-trained model on asl-now).

## Does asl-now help on your held-out takes?

Cross-validated by take: every take is held out once, and models never see frames from a held-out take. Probabilities are averaged over seeds 0, 1, 2.

| Training data | Mean accuracy (single model) | Per seed | Seed ensemble |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ours only | 87.0% | 85.7%, 90.1%, 85.1% | 87.9% |
| Ours + asl-now | 96.8% | 98.3%, 95.5%, 96.6% | 97.6% |

**Shipped: ours + asl-now** (combined did not lower mean held-out accuracy). Per-letter results below use the seed ensemble for stability.

## Other signers

- Trained on asl-now, tested on ours: 80.7%
